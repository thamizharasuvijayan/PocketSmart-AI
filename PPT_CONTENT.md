
# PPT — PocketSmart AI

## Slide 1 — Title
PocketSmart AI
Your Smart Budget & Recommendation Assistant
B.Sc. Computer Science Project

## Slide 2 — Introduction
- Personal finance tracking web application
- Records income and expenses
- Categorizes spending
- Provides budget recommendations

## Slide 3 — Problem
- Difficult to track daily spending
- Manual records can be inconsistent
- Users may not know which category consumes most money

## Slide 4 — Objectives
- Track transactions
- Set monthly budget
- Visualize spending
- Calculate balance
- Give smart recommendations

## Slide 5 — Modules
Authentication, Transaction Management, Budget Management, Dashboard, Recommendation Engine, Database

## Slide 6 — Technology
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python Flask, SQLite, Chart.js

## Slide 7 — System Flow
User → Dashboard → Transaction Entry → Database → Analysis → Recommendation → Visualization

## Slide 8 — Recommendation Logic
Budget overrun detection
Category spending analysis
Low-savings suggestion
Remaining-budget message

## Slide 9 — Expected Output
Dashboard with income, expenses, balance, budget, chart and recent transactions.

## Slide 10 — Future Scope
ML prediction, mobile app, notifications, PDF/Excel reports, recurring payments.

## Slide 11 — Conclusion
PocketSmart AI provides a simple way to track personal finances and understand spending behaviour through a single dashboard.
